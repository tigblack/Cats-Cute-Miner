# Cats Cute Miner Privacy Policy

Draft for review — 14 October 2026. This document is not published. Its descriptions must be checked against the final Android build before adoption. It applies only to Cats Cute Miner (`com.multicastgames.catscuteminer`), not every Multicast game.

Cats Cute Miner is provided by Multicast Games Limited. Questions and requests concerning privacy can be sent to **[confirm the studio's public support email before publication]**.

The game is intended for a mixed audience, including children. At first launch a neutral age screen determines the appropriate mode. The game keeps an age category rather than a full date of birth. An unknown age receives the protected mode. Selecting an older category does not provide an extra reward.

In protected mode, ordinary gameplay and progress saving work without an advertising or analytics account. AppLovin MAX and Meta SDKs are not initialized, and advertisements are not displayed. Optional analytics and attribution remain disabled for protected users. Save files and settings are stored on the device; deleting the app may remove them.

For eligible older users, optional services operate according to the actual choices recorded in the privacy settings and applicable consent requirements. AppMetrica supports gameplay analytics, Adjust supports attribution, Meta supports selected app events, and AppLovin MAX supports rewarded advertisements. Their final data categories and purposes must be listed here after the SDK and network configuration audit. Declining optional collection does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

Google Play processes monetary purchases. We do not receive card or bank account details. Multicast's purchase service uses limited transaction information and a game-specific account identifier to verify purchases, deliver crystals, prevent duplicate delivery, and recover unfinished transactions. Children need a parent's or guardian's permission for monetary purchases; applicable Google Play parental approval settings also apply. Purchase validation information must not be reused for child advertising or profiling.

You or a parent or guardian may contact us to ask about retained information, request deletion where applicable, or raise a concern about a child's data. Device-only progress can be removed through the game's confirmed reset flow or the device's app settings. Required transaction records may remain while needed for purchase support, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. The final retention periods, deletion procedure, and support contact must be confirmed before publication.

Material changes will be reflected in the date and text of this game-specific policy.
